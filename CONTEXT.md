# Aroma Gastro Bar SEO Context

This context defines the language used to describe Aroma Gastro Bar's local discovery, dining, nightlife, and content-maintenance model.

## Business identity and discovery

**Aroma Gastro Bar**:
The customer-facing name of the Orlando restaurant and lounge.
_Avoid_: Aroma Gastrobar, Aroma Gastro Bar Orlando (as the business name)

**Dominican-rooted Latin fusion**:
Aroma's cuisine position: Dominican heritage expressed through a broader Latin-fusion menu and contemporary dining experience.
_Avoid_: Generic Caribbean restaurant, Mexican restaurant

**Azalea Park**:
The local area descriptor used with Orlando when describing where Aroma is located or serves nearby guests.
_Avoid_: Mills 50, Downtown Orlando, or any neighborhood claim that is not confirmed by the business.

**Aroma Social**:
The name for Aroma's recurring nightlife and event programming.
_Avoid_: A permanent event schedule when a date or program has not been confirmed.

## Site and content language

**Canonical route**:
The preferred public URL for a useful Aroma page; alternate or legacy URLs point to it rather than competing with it.
_Avoid_: SEO landing page, doorway page

**Event series**:
A recurring nightlife program such as Game Night, Noche Toxica Friday, or R&B Saturdays, represented only by the dates and details currently confirmed.
_Avoid_: Evergreen event date, guaranteed weekly schedule

**Proof asset**:
Owner-approved evidence of the Aroma experience, such as a real testimonial, press mention, award, Google Business Profile link, or original photo.
_Avoid_: Copied review, fabricated award, unattributed quote

**Content source of truth**:
The owner-maintained set of approved business facts, menu highlights, event records, and proof assets used for public site content.
_Avoid_: Scraped content, unverified social post, stale page copy
